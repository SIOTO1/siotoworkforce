What we teach

Workplace safety

Spotting hazards, setting up and handling equipment safely, reporting incidents, working in bad weather, and safety walkthroughs for supervisors.

For crews, drivers, warehouse and field staff, and their leads.

Customer service

Phone and counter skills, handling complaints, fixing a bad experience, and following up the same way every time. Your team practices with real situations from your business.

For front desk, dispatch, delivery and service teams.

Sales

Following up on leads, asking the right questions, quoting, handling objections, and keeping a clean pipeline. New reps get coached until they hit their numbers.

For inside sales, account managers, and owners who sell.

Marketing and AI

Using AI tools to write, schedule and answer customers, plus the basics of local search and social media. We also set clear rules for keeping customer data safe.

For small business owners, office staff and marketing leads.

Your people practice. They don't just sit through slides.

We use real situations from your business and keep classes short enough to fit around shifts. Every class includes practice and role play. Supervisors leave with a simple checklist so the new habits stick after we're gone.

How it works

  1. We talk

    A short call about your team, your goals and where things go wrong today.

  2. You get a plan

    A written plan with topics, hours, number of trainees, schedule and cost.

  3. We train

    At your location, online, or in a group class with other employers in your area.

  4. You get the records

    Attendance sheets, hours and completion records for every trainee, ready for your files or a funding program.

Using public training funds?

Some workforce programs help employers pay for training. Each program sets its own rules on who qualifies and what it covers. We'll check those rules with the program before any training starts and keep records the way the program asks.
