Training tracks

Four tracks. Each one built for the people doing the work.

01

Workplace safety

Hazard awareness, safe setup and equipment handling, incident reporting, weather and site procedures, and supervisor safety walk-throughs.

For crews, drivers, warehouse and field staff, and their leads.

02

Customer service

Phone and counter skills, handling complaints, service recovery, and consistent follow-up, practiced through role-play on real scenarios from your business.

For front desk, dispatch, delivery, and service teams.

03

Sales

Lead follow-up, discovery questions, quoting, handling objections, and pipeline habits, coached so new reps reach quota faster.

For inside sales, account managers, and owners who sell.

04

Marketing and AI

Practical use of AI tools for writing, scheduling, and customer communication, plus local search and social basics, with clear rules for protecting customer data.

For small-business owners, office staff, and marketing leads.

Our approach

Practice, not slides.

  • Content tailored to your equipment, customers, and procedures
  • Short sessions that fit around shifts
  • Hands-on practice and role-play in every module
  • Supervisor checklists so new habits stick after training

How it works

From first call to finished training.

  1. Needs review

    A short call about your team, your goals, and where problems show up today.

  2. Training plan

    A written plan with topics, hours, number of trainees, schedule, and cost.

  3. Delivery

    On site, virtual, or as part of a shared cohort with other employers in your area.

  4. Records

    Sign-in sheets, hours, and completion records for every trainee, ready for your files or a funding program's reporting.

Using public training funds?

Some workforce programs help employers pay for training. Eligibility and approved uses depend on each program's rules. We'll help you document training the way the program requires, and confirm the rules with the program before any training begins.

About

Built by an operator, for operators.

SIOTO Workforce is the employer-training division of Safe Inflatable Operators Training Organization, Inc. (SIOTO), a safety training and certification company. Our curriculum comes from years of running Chicago service businesses with field crews, trucks, and customer-facing teams. We teach the safety, service, and sales systems we use every day.
